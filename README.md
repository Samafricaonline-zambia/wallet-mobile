# Sampay - Your All-in-One Payment Platform

Sampay is Zambia's leading digital payment gateway, empowering financial inclusion for everyone - from individuals to large enterprises. Send money, pay bills, buy airtime and electricity, make international transfers, and accept payments - all from one secure platform.

## Sampay Mobile App (Wallet)

This is the official mobile application for the **Sampay Wallet**. The app provides a convenient way for users to manage their finances on the go, offering seamless access to all Sampay services directly from their smartphones.
